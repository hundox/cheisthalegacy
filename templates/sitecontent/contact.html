A Living Archive

Memories, held in trust

An archive is never quite finished. Each page here records her schooling, her work and her friendships. The Foundation receives memories, photographs and stories of Cheistha and holds them as part of this record, with care and discretion.

You are welcome to add to it. A photograph kept from a school annual, a message still saved on your phone, a story told once across a dinner table: anything you have of Cheistha can be sent to the Foundation through the contact details above.
